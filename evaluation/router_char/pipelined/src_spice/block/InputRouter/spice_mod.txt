InputRouter: targeted fanout sizing (measured W-only sizing)
==============================================================
Only MOS W parameters were changed. L, devices, topology and ports are identical.
Keeper devices and their feedback inverters retain their generated dimensions.
Units: widths and lengths below are micrometres. Technology: PTM65 TT, 1.1 V, 27 C.
The largest trial widths are NMOS 1.56 um and PMOS 2.6 um (16x).
PRS source was not regenerated or changed. A future prs2net build would lose this manual sizing.
Original generated node reff comments describe the unsized baseline, not the new resistances.

Method
------
Gate loads counted after recursive instance/port expansion; repeated instances are included.
invEq is sum(W*L) / (0.26*0.065), a gate-area load proxy in minimum-inverter units, not extracted capacitance.
Targets: high-fanout control drivers and limited support for their existing upstream stages.
Width factors 2/4/8/16 are bounded trial choices; stronger drivers also increase upstream loading.
Logical-effort reference: https://pages.hmc.edu/harris/class/e158/10/lect6.pdf
No automatic timing optimum, layout/parasitic signoff or measured gain is implied by the sizing alone.

Changed MOS: 16 in definitions; 16/1491 after instance expansion (1.07%).

Sizing refinement
-----------------
Candidate v1 scaled only the large control root by 8x and passed functionally,
but increased handshake recovery. It is archived, not retained as the active netlist.
The retained candidate reduces that root to 4x and scales a few existing preceding drivers by 2x.
These support stages originally have modest fanout; their added gate load comes from the resized root.
No stage, wire or device was inserted or removed. See candidate_v1/ in the archive for the rejected measurements.
